# ncr-capa — Nonconformance & corrective action (L4; requires quality-inspection; optional party-master, lot-traceability, hr-core)

**Entities** NCR{number,source[iqc|ipqc|fqc|customer_complaint|audit|internal],item,lot,qty,defect_code,severity,detected_by,detected_at,containment_actions[],status[open|containment|investigation|disposition|closed],cost_estimate}; Disposition{type[use_as_is|rework|scrap|return_to_supplier|concession|sort],approvals,qty_split}; RootCause{method[5why|fishbone|8d],data(json),conclusion}; CAPA{type[corrective|preventive],actions[{desc,owner,due,status}],effectiveness_check{due,result},status}; SCAR{supplier,ncr_ref,response_due,response,status}; DefectCode tree; CostOfPoorQuality entries.
**Workflow** NCR: open→containment→investigation→disposition→closed; CAPA: planned→in_progress→verification→effective|ineffective(→reopen); SLAs by severity; SCAR sent via notifications.
**Rules** NCR can be created from events (`quality-inspection.result.out_of_spec`, `batch-formulation.deviation.raised`) as drafts; disposition triggers stock actions through events (scrap, return, rework WO) never direct DB; recurring defects detection by (item/supplier/defect) frequency threshold proposes CAPA; effectiveness check scheduled automatically; closure needs evidence attachments; costs summed to COPQ.
**Events out** ncr-capa.ncr.opened|dispositioned|closed, ncr-capa.capa.opened|verified, ncr-capa.scar.sent. **In** quality-inspection.result.out_of_spec, quality-inspection.lot.decided{reject}, batch-formulation.deviation.raised, calibration.out_of_tolerance.impact.
**Service contract** NcrService: openNcrs(filter), defectStats(range,groupBy), supplierNcrCount(supplier,range), capaDue(range).
**Permissions** ncr-capa.ncr|capa|scar.view|create|update|approve|close, ncr-capa.defectcode.manage.
**Screens** NCR board, NCR form (tabs: Details, Containment, Root cause, Disposition, Costs, Docs), CAPA tracker with due dates, 8D report print, Pareto of defects, supplier NCR scorecard, COPQ dashboard.
**AI** root-cause assistant (suggest causes from similar NCRs, read-only proposals), summarize, anomaly (recurrence), next_action (overdue CAPA nudges).
