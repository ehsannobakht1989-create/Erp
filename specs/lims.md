# lims — Laboratory management (L4; requires quality-inspection; optional item-master, inventory, calibration, document-mgmt)

**Entities** Sample{number,source[inspection_lot|production|stability|external],item,lot,quantity,container,received_at,received_by,storage,status[registered|assigned|in_test|review|approved|rejected|disposed],retention_until}; TestMethod{code,name,version,units,procedure_doc,equipment_class,reagents}; Specification{item|category,version,tests[{method,limits,unit,critical}]}; TestAssignment{sample,test,analyst,due,status}; Result{assignment,value|text,unit,calc_trace,instrument_ref,at,status[entered|reviewed|approved|oos]}; OosInvestigation{result,phase[lab|full],findings,outcome}; Reagent/Standard{item_ref,lot,expiry,status,opened_at}; Analyst qualification{method,valid_until}; CoaReport.
**Rules** sample creation from inspection lots (event) with auto test assignment by specification; results validated vs limits; formula calculations versioned and traceable; instrument must be in-calibration (CalibrationService::isValid) when linked; reagent/standard must be non-expired; OOS flow mandatory when result fails (blocks approval until investigation closed); two-person review for approvals (setting); COA generation from approved results; retention sample tracking; turnaround time KPIs.
**Events out** lims.sample.registered, lims.result.approved|oos, lims.coa.issued. **In** quality-inspection.lot.created, calibration.instrument.status_changed, inventory.* (reagent stock).
**Service contract** LimsService: resultsForLot(lot), isLabReleased(lot), pendingTests(analyst), tatStats(range).
**Permissions** lims.sample|test|result.view|enter|review|approve, lims.method|spec.manage, lims.oos.manage, lims.reagent.manage.
**Screens** lab worklist by analyst/instrument, sample login (scan/print labels), result entry grid with spec highlighting and calc preview, review/approve queue, OOS investigation form, reagent register with expiry alerts, COA print, TAT dashboard.
**AI** extract results from instrument reports/PDF text (doc-ocr, proposal), anomaly (data integrity: repeated values, edits after approval), summarize sample history, forecast lab load.
**Iran** استانداردهای ملی/بین‌المللی as references; Persian COA print.
